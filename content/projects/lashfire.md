LASH FIRE aimed to significantly reduce the risk of fires on board ro-ro ships by developing and validating technical, operational and regulatory fire safety solutions. RISE coordinated the EU Horizon 2020 project, which addressed the full fire protection chain, including ignition prevention, detection, extinguishment, containment, manual operations and human-centred system design. Within the project, RISE’s Dependable and Autonomous Systems unit developed and assessed an autonomous drone system for maritime firefighting and rescue support. The work included an open-source ground station prototype, Control Tower, based on open standards and the WayWise prototyping library, supporting use cases such as automated fire patrol, firefighting resource management and search and rescue. The system was demonstrated on board DFDS Petunia Seaways and evaluated through technical, legal and usefulness assessments, showing potential for improving situational awareness and emergency response while identifying remaining challenges around automated landing, weather robustness, regulatory approval and crew trust.
