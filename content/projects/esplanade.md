ESPLANADE focused on how to argue that an automated road vehicle is safe, addressing key safety assurance challenges that differ from manually driven vehicles. The project developed methods for human–ADS interaction and safe transitions of responsibility, risk assessment and safety-goal definition, Operational Design Domain reasoning, functional architecture development, sensor-related safety requirements, and safety contracts for component-based design and continuous deployment. RISE coordinated the FFI project, which brought together partners from vehicle manufacturers, suppliers, research organisations and expert companies. The results included new methods such as the Quantitative Risk Norm approach, safety analysis of transition protocols, ODD structuring for safety argumentation, and contract-based methods for requirements refinement and product-line safety assurance.
