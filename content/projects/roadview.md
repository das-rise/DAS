ROADVIEW aims to develop robust and cost-efficient in-vehicle perception and decision-making systems for connected and automated vehicles, with enhanced performance in harsh weather conditions such as snow, rain and fog, as well as in complex traffic scenarios. The project combines advanced sensing, adaptive sensor fusion, sensor noise modelling, collaborative perception, weather-aware decision-making and simulation-assisted testing to improve the reliability and safety of automated driving. The ROADVIEW solutions are integrated and demonstrated on vehicle platforms with the goal of reaching Technology Readiness Level 7. RISE participates as a project partner contributing to system requirements, standards, data quality, sensor robustness, trustworthy AI and evaluation activities.
