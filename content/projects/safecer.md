SafeCer was an ARTEMIS project family combining pSafeCer and nSafeCer, active overall from 2011 to 2015, focused on safety certification of software-intensive systems with reusable components. The project aimed to reduce certification cost and time-to-market by enabling composable safety certification, where safety arguments and evidence for components can be reused and integrated into system-level safety cases. The work covered safety contracts, certification properties, component meta-models, integrated development and certification processes, tool support, variant and product-line management, and cross-domain reuse across automotive, construction equipment, avionics, railway and other safety-critical domains. SP Technical Research Institute of Sweden, now part of RISE, participated in both pSafeCer and nSafeCer, contributing to methods, tool support, automotive demonstrators, implementation and integration, and dissemination.
