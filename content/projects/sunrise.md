SUNRISE aims to develop and demonstrate a harmonised and scalable safety assurance framework for connected, cooperative and automated mobility systems (CCAM). Building on HEADSTART and other initiatives, the project addresses scenario-based safety validation by combining harmonised assessment methodologies, virtual and physical testing, a federated European scenario database framework, and a test case library. The framework is intended to support industry, regulators, consumer testing organisations and other stakeholders in assessing the safety of automated driving systems. RISE participates as a Swedish project partner contributing to safety assurance methodology, scenario-based validation, simulation and hybrid/real-world testing activities.
