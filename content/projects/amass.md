AMASS developed an open European tool platform, ecosystem and methodology for assurance and certification of complex cyber-physical systems. The project built on earlier OPENCOSS and SafeCer results and focused on reducing certification effort through reusable assurance assets, architecture-driven assurance, multi-concern assurance covering safety, security and reliability, and interoperability between engineering, assurance and certification activities. SP Technical Research Institute of Sweden, now part of RISE, contributed mainly through a case study on multi-concern assurance, where a positioning unit for automated vehicles was analysed with respect to both functional safety and cybersecurity using AMASS tools and assurance-case methods.
