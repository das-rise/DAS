HEADSTART aimed to develop harmonised European methods for testing, validating and certifying connected and automated driving functions. The project focused on key enabling technologies such as positioning, vehicle communication and cybersecurity, and linked simulation, proving ground and real-world field testing to assess safety and security performance. HEADSTART brought together stakeholders from industry, academia and policy to support a common European approach for automated vehicle safety assessment. RISE participated as a Swedish project partner, contributing to the development of test methods for demonstrating that automated driving functions are sufficiently safe.
