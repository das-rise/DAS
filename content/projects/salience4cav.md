SALIENCE4CAV focused on how to ensure the safety of connected and automated vehicles while enabling frequent updates and continuous deployment of automated driving systems. The project addressed safety assurance, safety design, safe machine learning and variational safety across product lines and lifecycles, with results including methods for continuous safety cases, safety contracts, human interaction safety analysis, precautionary safety, minimal risk manoeuvre strategies, out-of-distribution detection and simulation-aided safety analysis of learning-enabled components. RISE coordinated the FFI project, and RISE’s Dependable and Autonomous Systems researchers contributed to work on continuous assurance, cooperative and collaborative automated vehicles, minimal risk manoeuvres, machine-learning safety and cross-domain safety concepts for road and confined-area automation.
