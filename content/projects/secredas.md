SECREDAS focused on making automated systems safer, more secure and more trustworthy across domains such as automotive, health and rail. The project developed methods, reference architectures and components that combine cybersecurity, privacy protection, functional safety and operational performance. RISE contributed expertise in safety and cybersecurity, including work on fault-injection preparation, threat and attack analysis, Bluetooth Low Energy security testing, and guidelines for multi-concern assessment and qualification. The results support development processes where safety, cybersecurity and privacy are considered together throughout the lifecycle of dependable automated system.
