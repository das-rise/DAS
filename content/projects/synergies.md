SYNERGIES aims to create a European scenario dataspace and marketplace for real and synthetic traffic scenarios supporting the development, training, virtual testing and validation of connected, cooperative and automated mobility systems. Building on the safety assurance framework from HEADSTART and SUNRISE, the project will connect and federate existing scenario databases, provide tools for data processing, scenario identification, scenario generation and database governance, and support scalable scenario-based validation of automated driving systems. RISE participates as a Swedish project partner contributing scenarios for Nordic conditions, both from recorded data and synthetically generated data, and explores generative AI for scenario generation and explainable AI for data analysis.
