ETAVEP, Enablers for Testing Autonomous Vehicles at Existing Proving grounds, was an FFI project coordinated by Volvo Cars and carried out from April 2020 to March 2022 together with AstaZero, Chalmers, RISE, SafeRadar Research and Volvo Group.  The project investigated how self-driven vehicles without a safety driver, cab or conventional driving controls can be tested safely at existing proving grounds without separating them completely from normal proving-ground operations. The project developed and demonstrated a proof-of-concept for replacing key safety functions normally performed by a test driver, including vehicle-status monitoring, emergency stopping, monitoring of the surrounding environment, traffic control, and updated proving-ground procedures.  RISE Dependable and Autonomous Systems especially contributed to the Safety Zone concept in the Traffic Control work, where a dynamic area is calculated around each self-driven vehicle based on vehicle dynamics, braking distance, turning radius, position uncertainty and surrounding objects.  If another object enters the safety zone, the traffic-control system sends a stop signal to the vehicle, enabling conservative but effective safety supervision for mixed testing environments.
