UNITED-GRID developed cyber-physical solutions for intelligent distribution grids with high penetration of renewable energy, including technologies for real-time grid awareness and control, forecasting, setting-less protection and integration with existing energy and distribution management systems. RISE contributed to the safety and cybersecurity aspects of the project by adapting the SAHARA method, originally developed for automotive systems, into a more general combined safety and cybersecurity risk assessment approach suitable for intelligent distributed grids. The method was applied to UNITED-GRID use cases such as advanced measurement solutions and setting-less protection, identifying critical assets, communication channels and threat scenarios where cybersecurity attacks could affect functional safety.
