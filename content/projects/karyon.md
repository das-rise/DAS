KARYON developed safety-kernel based architectures for predictable and safe coordination of autonomous mobile systems, such as cooperative road vehicles and unmanned aerial systems. The project addressed how complex autonomous control systems can cooperate in open and uncertain environments while maintaining safety, using a small local safety kernel to constrain behaviour and prevent hazardous situations. It also investigated fault detection concepts, especially for sensor systems, as well as middleware, simulation, mixed-reality and fault-injection tools for evaluating autonomous systems under failure conditions. SP Technical Research Institute of Sweden, now part of RISE, participated in the project, contributing to safety and dependability aspects for cooperative vehicle systems and safety assurance according to relevant standards.
