The Dependable and Autonomous Systems research unit is part of [RISE Research Institutes of Sweden](https://www.ri.se/).

We conduct applied research on safe, secure and trustworthy autonomous systems, primarily through collaborative national and international research projects. Our work develops methods, tools and prototypes for functional safety, cybersecurity, dependable AI, simulation, scenario-based validation and real-world testing.

Our expertise supports applications such as automated road vehicles, autonomous machines, drones, railways, maritime safety systems and intelligent energy grids. In addition to research projects, we support industry and public-sector partners with applied research, feasibility studies, prototyping, evaluations and expert analysis.
