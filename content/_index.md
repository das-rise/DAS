The Dependable and Autonomous Systems research unit is part of the [Research Institutes of Sweden](https://www.ri.se/).

We conduct research on safe, secure and trustworthy autonomous systems, primarily through collaborative national and international research projects. Our work develops methods, tools and prototypes for functional safety, cybersecurity, dependable AI, simulation, scenario-based validation and real-world testing, with applications in automated road vehicles, autonomous machines, drones, railways, maritime safety systems and intelligent energy  grids. In addition to research projects, we can also support commercial customers with applied research, feasibility studies, prototypes, evaluations and expert analysis.
